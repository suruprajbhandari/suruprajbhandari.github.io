Who I am

An incoming engineer, curious about technology and what can be built from an idea.

I'm an incoming Computer Engineering student at Kathmandu Engineering College, affiliated with Tribhuvan University. I'm based in Kathmandu, Nepal, and I'll be starting a four-year degree with a genuine interest in understanding how technology works — not just how to use it, but how it's designed and built.

My interests span several connected areas: technology and artificial intelligence, software development, and the broader question of how ideas become useful products. I'm drawn to startups and entrepreneurship, and to the intersection of technical knowledge and business thinking. I'm at the beginning of that journey, and I'm approaching it deliberately.

This site is an ongoing record of what I'm learning and building. It isn't a portfolio of finished achievements — it's a place to document the process honestly, share work as it develops, and establish a clear presence online as I start.
